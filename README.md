KiCad-Astra is a smart helper that works inside KiCad, the popular free PCB design software. ## 🧩 Key Features

### 🤖 AI-Assisted PCB Layout

KiCad-Astra looks at your components and suggests smart placements that make routing easier and your board more compact. It learns from best practices and applies them to your unique design.

### 🛤️ Smart Routing Help

Routing is the process of connecting components with copper traces. KiCad-Astra helps you route faster by suggesting efficient paths and avoiding common mistakes like sharp angles or traces that are too close together.

### ✅ Intelligent DRC (Design Rule Check)

Design Rule Check is like spell-check for your PCB. KiCad-Astra performs a thorough review and finds problems that a regular DRC might miss, such as potential manufacturing issues or electrical interference risks.

### 🏭 Fabrication Preparation

Before you send your design to a factory, KiCad-Astra reviews your files and prepares them properly. It checks things like board outline, copper clearances, and drill sizes so your board comes out right the first time.

### 🔒 Deterministic Local Checks

All the checks KiCad-Astra performs happen right on your computer. There is no cloud processing, meaning your designs stay private. The checks are "deterministic," which means they follow clear, logical rules you can understand and trust.

### 👤 Human Approval Always

KiCad-Astra never changes your design automatically. It shows you its suggestions with plain-English explanations, and you decide which ones to accept or ignore. You are always the boss.

## 🛠️ Example Workflow

Imagine you just placed all your components on a new board and are ready to connect them with copper traces.

1. You open KiCad-Astra and click "Analyze."
2. Astra notices that two capacitors are placed too far from the chip they support. It suggests moving them closer for better performance.
3. Astra also finds three traces that make sharp 90-degree angles, which can cause manufacturing issues. It suggests gentle 45-degree curves instead.
4. Finally, Astra spots that your board outline has a small gap that will confuse the manufacturer. It suggests closing the gap.
5. You read each suggestion, agree with all of them, and click "Fix All."
6. Your board now follows industry best practices, and you are ready to proceed with confidence.

### The Download Seems Slow

GitHub downloads can sometimes be slow depending on your internet connection. Be patient, and avoid closing the browser tab until the download completes.

This warning appears for many useful tools. It just means the publisher hasn't gone through a paid verification process. KiCad-Astra is safe to use, and you can click "More Info" and then "Run Anyway" to proceed.

### Astra Doesn't Analyze My Board
